1. Statutory Rental Expense Deduction (20%)

Under Cyprus tax legislation, landlords are automatically entitled to a flat 20% deduction on gross rental income for maintenance, repairs, and wear-and-tear before income tax is assessed (i.e., Taxable Rental Net Profit = Gross Annual Rent × 80%).

2. Individual Marginal Tax Brackets (Progressive Stacking - 2026)

For individuals, income tax is progressive. Adding rental income to existing earnings can push you into higher marginal tax tiers. The calculator uses exact marginal rate stacking:

  • €0 – €22,000: 0%
  • €22,001 – €32,000: 20%
  • €32,001 – €42,000: 25%
  • €42,001 – €72,000: 30%
  • Over €72,000: 35%

3. G.E.S.Y. (General Healthcare System) Contributions

Cyprus imposes a 2.65% G.E.S.Y. contribution calculated on the gross rental income for individuals (capped at €180,000 annual income). Corporate entities are exempt from G.E.S.Y. on rental profits.

4. Corporate Structure Alternative (15% Flat Rate)

If structured under a corporate entity, rental profits are subject to a flat 15% corporate tax on the net rental profit (after the 20% allowance), bypassing personal progressive slabs and G.E.S.Y.

5. Agent Fees & Commission VAT

Agent commissions are applied as a one-time Year 1 deduction. If VAT is toggled on, a 19% Cyprus VAT rate is added directly to the commission sum.
